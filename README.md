# Escape From Temu 
### By group 1.1


## Overview

**Escape From Temu** is a terminal-based adventure game created in C# using the delivered code from **World of Zuul**. This game has been redesigned to incorporate ASCII art to represent rooms and uses key-based inputs instead of text-based commands for player interaction. The goal of the game is to escape from a factory as a slave, by conversing with NPCs to gain information, this increase the player's score.

The project is a fusion of the **World of Zuul** game and a focus on slave reduction, inspired by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) of reducing slavery. This theme has been integrated into the game's narrative and objectives.

## Gameplay

- **Movement**: Use the keys (w a s d) to move around, in between rooms.
- **Interactions**: Press the (i) key to interact with people and pick up items.
- **Score**: Talk to people and listen to their story, more stories are unlocked by finding sentimental items 


## Goals and Themes

This game is designed with the goal of contributing to the broader objective of **reducing modern slavery**, as outlined by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 8:

### **SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth**
   - **Target 8.7**: Immediate and effective measures must be taken to eradicate forced labor, modern slavery, and human trafficking, and to ensure the prohibition and elimination of the worst forms of child labor, including the recruitment and use of child soldiers, and to eradicate child labor in all its forms by 2025.

The game's narrative centers around a slave escaping from a factory, highlighting the life of those trapped in modern slavery. By raising awareness through experience, this project aims to contribute to the global effort to achieve Target 8.7.
